Who we are

Compassion turned into action

Angel Heart Foundation is being established to help improve the lives of vulnerable children across Africa. We envision strong communities where children are nourished, protected, educated, and supported by caring families.

Our mission

To restore smiles and support vulnerable children through essential assistance, educational opportunities, and family-centered protection.

Our vision

An Africa where every child grows up in safety and dignity, with opportunities to thrive.
